### Which compression-window and quantized-cache contracts must travel together?

`attention-compression-cache-contract` · constraint · evidence: **source-only** · applies: lang flydsl

**When:** The chosen source path is the fused compression/cache preparation kernel, not ordinary dense attention.
**Try:**
- Preserve the overlap setting: the builder uses K = 2*ratio for overlap and K = ratio without overlap, with a state-size requirement.
- Select the cache output format explicitly: per-row FP8, grouped FP8 and FP4 have different scales and layouts.
- Enforce the builder's vector-width, rotary-dimension, state-size and FP4 alignment checks before tuning its pipeline.
**Why:** The builder couples compression geometry, online rescaling, quantization and cache scatter in one implementation.
**Alternatives:** Keep compression and quantized cache packing separate until both intermediate outputs agree with the reference.
**Limits:**
- This source is a compression/preparation stage; it is not proof of a complete compressed-attention serving path.
- FP4 and FP8 scale contracts are not interchangeable.
**Reported:**
- gain reported: PR #3357 introduces the compressor contracts and reports a range of HCA split-path gains; the performance claim does not cover every CSA/FP8 variant.
