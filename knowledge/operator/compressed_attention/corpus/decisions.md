### Which compression-window and quantized-cache contracts must travel together?

**Card:** `attention-compression-cache-contract` · **Type:** `constraint` · **Evidence:** `source_observed`

**Axes:** `compression`, `cache_layout`, `numerical_contract`

#### Applicability

- `operator_families`: <code>compressed_attention</code>
- `target_languages`: <code>flydsl</code>

#### Use when

- The chosen source path is the fused compression/cache preparation kernel, not ordinary dense attention.

#### Try

- Preserve the overlap setting: the builder uses K = 2*ratio for overlap and K = ratio without overlap, with a state-size requirement.
- Select the cache output format explicitly: per-row FP8, grouped FP8 and FP4 have different scales and layouts.
- Enforce the builder's vector-width, rotary-dimension, state-size and FP4 alignment checks before tuning its pipeline.

#### Rationale

- The builder couples compression geometry, online rescaling, quantization and cache scatter in one implementation.

#### Keep as alternatives

- Keep compression and quantized cache packing separate until both intermediate outputs agree with the reference.
