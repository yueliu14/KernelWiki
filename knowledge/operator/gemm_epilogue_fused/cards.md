How to read a card:
- **Evidence** — `reported`: an upstream PR or benchmark reported an outcome (not reproduced here; the outcome
  line says what was reported). `source-only`: observed in shipped source or configs; it shows what an
  implementation does, not that it wins. Treat both as hypotheses and measure on your own kernel and shapes.
- **Applies** — the languages/dtypes/GPUs the card was derived from. For a kernel in another language the card is a
  porting idea, not a recipe.
- `constraint` cards are correctness or validity rules: check them before trusting a speedup.

Design traits that some cards require (`requires`):
- `lds_staging` — The design stages at least one operand tile through LDS: it is stored to LDS and the MFMA fragments are read back from it.
- `reused_weight` — B, the weight, is reused across calls, so a one-time host-side layout transform such as a preshuffle is paid once per weight rather than per call.
- `split_k_hot_loop` — The K loop is hand-scheduled with ROCm scheduling directives over LDS-staged operands, as the FlyDSL split-K HGEMM hot loop is.

### Which fusion boundary helps each regime of a normalization/GEMM chain?

`fused-gemm-regime-specific-chain` · performance · evidence: **reported** · applies: lang all

**When:** The chain has distinct prefill and decode execution arms and explicit intermediate/output ownership.
**Try:**
- Measure the full chain and each arm before selecting a fusion; preserve normalization, casts, residual updates and output initialization.
- For compute-bound arms, inspect native cast and packed arithmetic lowering; for short arms, compare the removed dispatch cost to added synchronization and resource pressure.
- Keep a change conditional on the regime where its complete effect is beneficial.
**Why:** Historical composed norm/GEMM reports combined dispatch reduction with prefill arithmetic changes; further overlap and cache-load hints could regress a roof-bound arm.
**Alternatives:** Keep the existing correct implementation as the baseline and fallback; compare changes on the same workload.
**Limits:** A fused call is not proof of a single device launch. Changes to cast placement or normalization need an independent numerical check.
**Reported:**
- mixed: Historical composed norm/GEMM reports combined dispatch reduction with prefill arithmetic changes; further overlap and cache-load hints could regress a roof-bound arm.

### When can a FlyDSL GEMM route its accumulators through an LDS CShuffle epilogue, and what does it cost?

`flydsl-cshuffle-epilogue` · performance · evidence: **source-only** · applies: lang flydsl

**When:** target_language=flydsl; the output store path (layout, vector width, atomics) is being chosen
**Try:**
- Treat the epilogue as a choice between the row epilogue, which stores straight from the MFMA accumulator layout, and the LDS CShuffle epilogue, which writes the tile row-major into LDS, barriers, re-reads it with threads remapped to (MLane, NLane) = (8, 32) and emits vectorised half2 stores or atomics.
- Check legality first: block_size must divide by cshuffle_nlane (32), tile_m by CShuffleMLane (256/32 = 8 for the 256-thread A8 block), and tile_n by cshuffle_nlane*e_vec (64 by default).
- Price the LDS: the staging buffer is 2*tile_m*tile_n bytes and shares the A ping/pong space in the family's estimate; when it overflows, the helper can split the buffer between two wave groups.
- Use its `store_pair` hook for split-K or atomic accumulation: the same shuffled layout feeds either a store or an atomic.
- Keep both arms: in AITER's gfx950 fp8 selections CShuffle on and off both occur in every M bucket (22 of 72 rows on at M<=16, 32 of 50 at M 1025-4096).
- In the split-K HGEMM the equivalent (`c_to_lds`) is fixed off and rejected if requested.
**Why:**
- The helper states its legality as raises and its data flow in its docstring, so the contract is readable rather than reverse-engineered.
- The epilogue is the only place the corpus has no other card for, and it is where store coalescing and split-K accumulation are decided.
**Alternatives:** Direct row store from the MFMA layout — the default and the control arm.
**Limits:**
- Legality is the helper's; the LDS cost interacts with the A staging budget, so re-run the LDS estimate for the combined configuration.
- Selection shares are where AITER chose each arm, not how much either arm helped.
**Reported:**
- no result: PR #2546 incorporates this choice into the A8 preshuffle path; no quantitative benefit for the individual mechanism is reported.
**Existing implementations:** ck: the CShuffle epilogue is part of the device-op template (`CShuffleDataType`, `*_Xdl_CShuffle*` instances)
**Read more:** [flydsl.md:20-26](backend/flydsl.md), [authoring_gemm_levers.md:167-180](../../general/backend/flydsl/authoring_gemm_levers.md)

### Where does an affine epilogue belong when the GEMM is split over K?

`gemm-blockscale-fused-affine-split` · semantics · evidence: **source-only** · applies: lang triton

**When:** The selected implementation matches the cited path and its input contract.
**Try:**
- Keep FUSE_TYPE=0 (a*GEMM+b) distinct from the alternate form (b*a+GEMM), including scalar versus strided tensor operands.
- For NUM_KSPLIT=1 the main kernel applies the epilogue; for split execution the reduce kernel sums partials before applying it once.
- Do not apply the additive term independently to every partial, which would multiply that term by the number of splits.
**Why:** The cited source declares these operations and checks; the combination is part of its interface.
**Alternatives:** Keep a simpler implementation with the identical numerical and layout contract for comparison.
**Limits:** This is an implementation precedent, not a measured performance preference on a new workload.
