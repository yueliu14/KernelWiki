- `constraint` cards are correctness or validity rules: check them before trusting a speedup.

### Can expert-weight dequantization be reused or shortened on the critical path?

`moe-dequant-reuse-and-chain` · performance · evidence: **reported** · applies: lang all

**When:** Packed expert weights or scale metadata are converted repeatedly while feeding grouped GEMM.
**Try:**
- Load a packed value once when extracting multiple components; consider reusing a dequantized weight tile across M blocks only when expert and scale ownership match.
- Distinguish widening the number of dot tiles from widening each tile: compiler warp-layout changes can duplicate the very work being removed.
- Inspect scale gathers and dependent conversion chains, then re-tune tile grouping within register/LDS budgets.
**Why:** Historical reports found useful packed extraction, shared dequantized tiles and shorter scale/convert chains; nominal instruction savings could be off the critical path or disappear after retiling.
**Alternatives:** Keep the existing correct implementation as the baseline and fallback; compare changes on the same workload.
**Limits:** Preserve group-scale application and cancellation-sensitive arithmetic. Extra dequantized weight copies must be included in deployment memory accounting.
**Reported:**
- mixed: Historical reports found useful packed extraction, shared dequantized tiles and shorter scale/convert chains; nominal instruction savings could be off the critical path or disappear after retiling.

<a id="moe-two-stage-intermediate"></a>
### What must match at the gate/up to down-projection boundary?

`moe-two-stage-intermediate` · semantics · evidence: **source-only** · applies: lang flydsl/triton

**When:** MoE execution uses two expert GEMMs with an activation or gate between them.
**Try:**
- Preserve gate/up ordering, the exact activation and any clipping before the down projection.
- Match the intermediate dtype, quantization scale layout and sorted-route ownership across both stages.
- Choose stage-1 split/tiling and stage-2 consumption together; a fast stage-1 output is unusable if stage 2 interprets it differently.
- Consider the complete expert call and its metadata preparation when sizing a fusion or rewrite; preserve routed weights, activation order, layouts and intermediate lifetimes.
- Check reduction order and deterministic-output requirements when adding split-K or moving work between stages.
**Why:** The FlyDSL family has distinct gate/up and down builders, including separate dequantization and scale helpers.
**Alternatives:** Keep the intermediate in a simple floating format before introducing compressed partials.
**Limits:**
- These FP8 family precedents do not establish arbitrary MXFP4 or mixed-expert-weight compatibility.
- A dense-GEMM replacement may not match a fused expert callable. Combined serving improvements cannot be attributed to either GEMM without ablations.
**Reported:**
- mixed: Historical MoE reports found metadata/auxiliary-dispatch removal useful after tuning the GEMMs, while a fast order-changing rewrite failed serving parity.
**Existing implementations:** triton: The grouped GEMM exposes activation and quantization parameters in its own kernel contract.
