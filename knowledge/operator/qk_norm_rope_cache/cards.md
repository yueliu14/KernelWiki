### What must a Q/K normalization, RoPE and cache-write fusion preserve?

`attention-qk-rope-cache-fusion` · performance · evidence: **source-only** · applies: lang flydsl/hip/gluon

**When:** Q/K preparation produces data consumed by attention or an indexer cache.
**Try:**
- Keep normalization weights/epsilon, rotary pair convention, positional indices and cache slot mapping identical to the unfused path.
- For the wave64 builder, enforce its even rotary dimension and vector divisibility requirements before attempting packed FP8 stores.
- Separate model-lifetime tensor adapters from request-lifetime tensors: the source caches static weights/cos/sin, while request tensors must not be retained as static adapters.
**Why:** The implementation couples norm/RoPE with vectorized stores and contains separate wave32/wave64 builders and static-adapter lifetime handling.
**Alternatives:** Keep a sequence of norm, RoPE and cache write operations as the same-contract baseline.
**Limits:**
- The constraints cited for the wave64 builder do not automatically describe the wave32 builder.
- A successful fusion must match the consumer's quantization and cache layout, not only its Q/K values.

### What must remain intact when a batched projection is fused with RoPE and MLA cache writes?

`bmm-rope-cache-fused-write-contract` · performance · evidence: **source-only** · applies: lang triton

**When:** The selected implementation matches the cited path and its input contract.
**Try:**
- Preserve the distinction between the projected content portion, rotary portion and the final cache concatenation layout.
- Carry the slot/page indices and all cache strides through the fused write; the batch/head projection index is not a physical cache slot.
- For split FP4 projection, account for the separate reduction before treating the projection as a completed value.
**Why:** The cited source declares these operations and checks; the combination is part of its interface.
**Alternatives:** Keep a simpler implementation with the identical numerical and layout contract for comparison.
**Limits:**
- A BMM fusion belongs to the Q/K cache-preparation contract as well as the projection; copying only its dot loop is incomplete.
- No new speedup is established by the fused source.
