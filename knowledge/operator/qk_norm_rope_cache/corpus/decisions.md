### What must a Q/K normalization, RoPE and cache-write fusion preserve?

**Card:** `attention-qk-rope-cache-fusion` · **Type:** `performance_candidate` · **Evidence:** `source_observed`

**Axes:** `cache_layout`, `numerical_contract`, `data_movement`

#### Applicability

- `operator_families`: <code>qk_norm_rope_cache</code>
- `target_languages`: <code>flydsl</code>, <code>hip</code>, <code>gluon</code>

#### Use when

- Q/K preparation produces data consumed by attention or an indexer cache.

#### Try

- Keep normalization weights/epsilon, rotary pair convention, positional indices and cache slot mapping identical to the unfused path.
- For the wave64 builder, enforce its even rotary dimension and vector divisibility requirements before attempting packed FP8 stores.
- Separate model-lifetime tensor adapters from request-lifetime tensors: the source caches static weights/cos/sin, while request tensors must not be retained as static adapters.

#### Rationale

- The implementation couples norm/RoPE with vectorized stores and contains separate wave32/wave64 builders and static-adapter lifetime handling.

#### Keep as alternatives

- Keep a sequence of norm, RoPE and cache write operations as the same-contract baseline.

### What must remain intact when a batched projection is fused with RoPE and MLA cache writes?

**Card:** `bmm-rope-cache-fused-write-contract` · **Type:** `performance_candidate` · **Evidence:** `source_observed`

**Axes:** `cache_layout`, `numerical_contract`

#### Applicability

- `operator_families`: <code>qk_norm_rope_cache</code>
- `target_languages`: <code>triton</code>

#### Use when

- The selected implementation matches the cited path and its input contract.

#### Try

- Preserve the distinction between the projected content portion, rotary portion and the final cache concatenation layout.
- Carry the slot/page indices and all cache strides through the fused write; the batch/head projection index is not a physical cache slot.
- For split FP4 projection, account for the separate reduction before treating the projection as a completed value.

#### Rationale

- The cited source declares these operations and checks; the combination is part of its interface.

#### Keep as alternatives

- Keep a simpler implementation with the identical numerical and layout contract for comparison.
