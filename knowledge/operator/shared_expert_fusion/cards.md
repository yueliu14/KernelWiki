### When may a fused collective consume the shared-expert partial?

`moe-shared-expert-readiness` · constraint · evidence: **source-only** · applies: lang flydsl

**When:** The fused runner is configured to add a shared-expert output produced on a potentially separate path.
**Try:**
- Preserve the explicit readiness dependency before the first operation that consumes shared_partial.
- Keep add_shared and output ownership consistent with gather versus reduce-scatter modes; ensure the shared contribution is added exactly once.
- Validate ragged/padded shared-partial preparation before overlapping it with routed-expert computation.
**Why:** The host orchestration has dedicated shared-partial preparation and wait_for_shared_if_used handling around collective consumption.
**Alternatives:** Synchronize and add the shared expert in a separate step as a correctness baseline.
**Limits:**
- A shared-expert speedup cannot be inferred from the presence of an overlap callback.
- Communication topology and scheduling are outside a single expert GEMM's contract.
**Reported:**
- mixed: PR #5907 adds shared-partial readiness and layout contracts in a wider comm-fused runtime; operator and DPA serving gains remain bundle results with family-specific numerical and reuse constraints.
