### When may a fused collective consume the shared-expert partial?

**Card:** `moe-shared-expert-readiness` · **Type:** `constraint` · **Evidence:** `source_observed`

**Axes:** `shared_expert`, `communication`, `combine`

#### Applicability

- `operator_families`: <code>shared_expert_fusion</code>, <code>fused_moe_grouped_gemm</code>
- `target_languages`: <code>flydsl</code>

#### Use when

- The fused runner is configured to add a shared-expert output produced on a potentially separate path.

#### Try

- Preserve the explicit readiness dependency before the first operation that consumes shared_partial.
- Keep add_shared and output ownership consistent with gather versus reduce-scatter modes; ensure the shared contribution is added exactly once.
- Validate ragged/padded shared-partial preparation before overlapping it with routed-expert computation.

#### Rationale

- The host orchestration has dedicated shared-partial preparation and wait_for_shared_if_used handling around collective consumption.

#### Keep as alternatives

- Synchronize and add the shared expert in a separate step as a correctness baseline.
