## Persistent mono decode introduced

**Before:** Ordinary vLLM attention, mHC/FFN seam, MoE and TP collective boundaries.

**After:** VLLM_ROCM_MONO_DECODE=1 selects two persistent FlyDSL launches for eligible standard layers; other layers use ordinary attention plus a fused FFN launch.

**Device language:** Triton/AITER/library stages -> persistent FlyDSL K1/K2 with Python dispatch.

**Body change:** New mono_decode.py and mono/{layer,runner,attention,stages} implement peer-memory handshakes, register/LDS staging and in-kernel TP reductions on loaded MXFP8/MXFP4 weights.

**Fusion:** K1 fuses attention seam (slice/gate/norm-to-MXFP8) with attention front. K2 fuses attention back/wo_b peer push, TP reduction + FFN seam, MoE and its TP reduction. It is two launches. First/Engram/compressor/indexer layers retain ordinary attention and take one FFN-only launch.

**Applicability:** MI355X/gfx950, TP2/TP4, EP1, decode-only, at most 48 active/padded token rows, with AITER A8W4-compatible weights and supported SWA/cache metadata. Five drafts can yield six rows per request; client concurrency is not the runtime row count. Larger/mixed steps take ordinary vLLM paths. Initial whole-layer path is standard seam, compression ratio 1/2, no local compressor/indexer; no EP/DP/EPLB or compiled path.
