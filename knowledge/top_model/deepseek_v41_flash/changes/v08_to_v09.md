## DSpark draft layers join mono decode

**Before:** Whole-backbone path exists, but draft layer IDs are excluded.

**After:** Draft layers can use a dedicated runner for their 128-expert/top-3 routing and non-causal SWA block; target remains 384-expert/top-6.

**Device language:** FlyDSL -> FlyDSL.

**Body change:** Parameterizes MonoBuild/scratch/runner by experts, topk, ug_groups and swa_width; draft runner has its own peer handshake and routing shape.

**Fusion:** Existing attention/FFN/MoE/collective fusion now also covers supported draft layers. Compressor/indexer remain external; this does not fuse five sequential draft sampling steps into one launch.

**Applicability:** MI355X/gfx950, TP2/TP4, EP1, decode-only, at most 48 active/padded token rows, with AITER A8W4-compatible weights and supported SWA/cache metadata. Five drafts can yield six rows per request; client concurrency is not the runtime row count. Larger/mixed steps take ordinary vLLM paths. Draft-specific non-causal metadata and routing/weight guards apply.
