### mHC dispatch and stream collapse

**Before:** AMD model directly calls mhc_pre_delayed_torch and mhc_post_torch; final/cross-layer HC collapse uses PyTorch multiply/reduce.

**After:** Model calls MHCPreDelayedOp and MHCPostOp; supported ROCm shapes dispatch to AITER. hc_collapse_triton replaces explicit HC multiply/reduce.

**Device language:** PyTorch/reference operations -> Triton HC collapse plus AITER calls; TileLang/reference fallbacks remain. Historical AITER device bytes for V02 were not recovered.

**Code/body change:** Replace reference call sites with platform CustomOps. Delayed AITER pre requires x=None, no fused norm, hc_mult=4 and hidden width divisible by 256; entry broadcast can fall back.

**Fusion:** HC weighted collapse has a fused Triton implementation. Post and delayed-pre are still separate call sites, and following RMSNorm remains separate; do not claim the entire mHC seam became one launch.

**Applicability to this pair:** MI355X/gfx950 ordinary vLLM path. No mono decode is enabled in these endpoint commands. Actual token rows and backend dispatch still determine the kernels executed.
