| [TP4 MoE finalize + all-reduce introduced](../changes/v01_to_v03_tp4_ep4.md#kernel-4) | New endpoint TEP4/EP4 and compact-A4 enabled; supported row count, top-8, hidden 4096 and local-expert predicates apply. The CLI does not enable the AMD layer monokernel. |

| [Residual/norm boundary with FP8 output](../changes/v01_to_v03_tp4_ep4.md#kernel-6) | Ordinary path when the model requests fp8_out/forward_fp8; do not transfer this benefit to the monokernel-covered layers without a trace. |
